16. CRITICAL WORKER BUSY BUSINESS RULE
======================================================================

User explicitly specified:

When Worker receives/accepts a job and works on it:
- Worker must become unavailable on the map.
- ALL of that Worker's other job categories/skills must show Busy.
- Worker cannot appear as freely available for another job during active work.

When the hired work finishes:
- All skills/categories become available again.

Therefore worker availability is GLOBAL to the worker while an active job is
running, even though the worker may have many categories and skills.

The manual availability toggle exists, but job lifecycle must automatically
control Busy/Available state as appropriate.

24. TWO HIRING MODES
======================================================================

Mode 1 — MANUAL / DIRECT HIRE
-----------------------------
- Show eligible nearby workers as map pins/list.
- Hirer selects a specific worker.
- Opens worker profile.
- Sends Direct Hire request to that worker.

Mode 2 — AUTO HIRE
-------------------
- User does NOT need to select a worker manually.
- Find Worker screen contains an Auto Hire button.
- Auto Hire orders eligible workers primarily by nearest distance.
- Request/ringing is sent to nearest worker first.
- Request has a limited acceptance/respond time.
- If worker does not accept/respond in time:
  automatically move to the next nearest eligible worker.
- Continue sequentially until a worker accepts or candidates are exhausted.

This is intended to behave similarly to Uber-style sequential dispatch.
